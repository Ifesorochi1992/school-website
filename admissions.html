Admissions

Take the first step towards a brighter future with us.

JOIN OUR SCHOOL COMMUNITY

Begin Your Learning Journey

Special Science and Art School Enugu welcomes families who value academic excellence, creativity and good character. We invite parents and guardians to make enquiries about admission into our Nursery, Primary and Secondary programmes.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Admission Requirements

Nursery Admission

  • Child's birth certificate
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Previous school record, if applicable
  • Parent or guardian contact details

Primary Admission

  • Birth certificate
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Previous academic report, if applicable
  • Transfer documents, where applicable

Secondary Admission

  • Birth certificate
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Previous school academic records
  • Transfer documents, where applicable

HOW TO APPLY

Our Admission Process

01

Make an Enquiry

Contact the school to request information about available classes and admission requirements.

02

Obtain an Application Form

Request an admission form and provide the necessary information about the student.

03

Prepare Required Documents

Gather the student's photographs, birth certificate and relevant academic records.

04

School Review

The school reviews the submitted information and communicates the next steps to the family.

DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Documents to Prepare

Parents and guardians should prepare the following documents when making an admission enquiry:

✓ Student's birth certificate

✓ Recent passport photographs

✓ Previous school report, where applicable

✓ Transfer certificate, where applicable

✓ Parent or guardian contact information
